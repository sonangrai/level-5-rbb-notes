This note covers syllabus item **5.6 Unified Directives / Circulars issued by
Nepal Rastra Bank for Commercial Banks**.

## What they are

- The **Unified Directives** (*Ekikrit Nirdeshan*) are NRB's **combined set of
  prudential and operational rules** for BFIs. NRB issues them under its
  powers in the **[NRB Act](/docs/nrb-act-2058)** and
  **[BAFIA](/docs/bafia-2073)**.
- NRB issues **separate** Unified Directives for **Class A** (commercial
  banks), **Classes B and C** (development banks and finance companies) and
  **Class D** (microfinance institutions).
- They are **reissued every year**, usually after the **Monetary Policy** is
  announced, and named by year, for example **"Unified Directives, 2081"**.

## Circulars

- During the year, NRB issues **circulars** (*paripatra*) that **add to,
  change or relax** specific directives.
- Circulars also put into effect the measures in the **Monetary Policy**, such
  as changes to the CRR, the CD ratio or the policy rate.
- The changes made by circulars are **merged into** the next year's Unified
  Directives.
- Banks must **comply from the date a circular takes effect**. They do not
  wait for the next Unified Directives.

## Enforcement

- NRB checks compliance through **on-site inspection** and **off-site
  supervision**.
- When a bank breaks a directive, NRB can **issue a warning**, **impose a
  fine**, **restrict business** (for example, stop dividends or lending),
  **act against directors and managers**, or **take over management** of the
  bank.

## How to study this topic

The figures change **every year**, so:

- Read the **latest Unified Directives** and the **circulars** issued since
  then. Both are on **NRB's website** (nrb.org.np).
- Learn **the current numbers** for capital ratios, provisioning rates, CRR,
  SLR, the CD ratio, the spread limit and the single-obligor limit.
- Read the **current Monetary Policy**, because it explains **why** the
  numbers changed.

## Quick revision

> [!TIP]
> **One-line answers.** The Unified Directives are NRB's **combined
> regulations** for BFIs. There are **separate** sets for **Class A, B/C and
> D**. They are **reissued every year**. **Circulars** amend them during the
> year and are merged into the next edition. Main areas: **capital,
> provisioning, single-obligor limit, liquidity, interest rates, sectoral
> lending, governance, credit information, AML/CFT and reporting**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Traps that show up in multiple-choice questions: a **circular** applies
> **from the date it takes effect**, not from the next Unified Directives;
> the single-obligor limit is measured against **core capital**, not total
> deposits; the Unified Directives come from **NRB**, not the Ministry of
> Finance.

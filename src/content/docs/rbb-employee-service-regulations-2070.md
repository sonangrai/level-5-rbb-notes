This note covers syllabus item **5.5 राष्ट्रिय वाणिज्य बैंक कर्मचारी सेवा
विनियमावली, २०७०** (**Rastriya Banijya Bank Employees Service Regulations,
2070**).

> [!CAUTION]
> **This note is an outline, not a summary of the regulations.** It shows the
> areas that RBB's service regulations cover and what to look for in each. The
> **specific provisions** (numbers of days, years, ages, levels and
> committees) are **not filled in yet**. Read them from the regulations
> themselves, which are available from RBB.

## What the regulations are

**Service regulations** (*sewa viniyamawali*) are the bank's own **rules for
its employees**. RBB's **Board of Directors** issues them, using its powers
under the **[Bank and Financial Institution Act](/docs/bafia-2073)** and the
**[Companies Act](/docs/companies-act-2063)**. They set out everything about
a staff member's career, from **joining** to **retirement**. They must give
staff at least the protections of the
**[Labour Act](/docs/labour-act-2074)**.

## Areas to study

### 1. Preliminary

- **Short title and commencement**: when the regulations came into force.
- **Definitions**: such as "employee", "service", "appointing authority" and
  "salary".

### 2. Organisation and service structure

- The bank's **service groups** (for example, administration and technical)
  and **levels and posts**.
- How the **number of posts** is set and changed.

### 3. Recruitment and appointment

- **Methods of filling posts**: open competition, internal competition and
  promotion, with the **share** of posts filled by each.
- **Eligibility**: **minimum qualifications** and **age limits** for each
  level.
- **Selection**: **written examination**, **interview** and the body that
  conducts them.
- **Appointing authority** for each level.
- **Probation**: its **length** and how an employee is **confirmed**
  (made permanent).

### 4. Pay and benefits

- **Salary scale** and **grade** increments.
- **Allowances**, **provident fund**, **gratuity or pension**, **insurance**
  and **staff loans**.

### 5. Promotion

- **Minimum service period** needed for promotion.
- **Basis for promotion**: **seniority**, **performance appraisal**,
  **education**, **training** and **work in remote areas**, with the **marks**
  given for each.
- The **promotion committee** and the process for **complaints** about
  promotion.

### 6. Performance appraisal

- Who **evaluates** whom (the supervisor and the reviewer), and **when** in
  the year.
- How the appraisal is used for **promotion and rewards**.

### 7. Transfer and posting

- **Transfer policy**: how long a person stays in one post, and transfers to
  **remote** areas.
- The **authority** that orders transfers.

### 8. Leave

- **Types of leave**: home, sick, maternity, paternity, mourning, study and
  extraordinary leave.
- **Number of days** for each, and how much can be **accumulated** or
  **encashed**.

### 9. Training and development

- How training and **study leave** are granted, and the **service bond**
  after training.

### 10. Code of conduct

- **Punctuality**, **discipline** and **confidentiality** of bank and customer
  information.
- **Restrictions** on political activity, on taking gifts or loans from
  customers, and on other business or employment.
- Declaring **property**, and avoiding **conflicts of interest**.

### 11. Departmental action

- **Minor penalties**, such as a warning or withheld increment, and **major
  penalties**, such as demotion or dismissal.
- **Procedure**: **clarification**, a chance to **defend oneself**, and the
  **authority** that imposes each penalty.
- **Suspension** during an investigation.
- **Appeal** against a penalty.

### 12. Retirement and separation

- **Compulsory retirement age**.
- **Voluntary retirement**, **resignation** and **termination**.
- **Retirement benefits**.
